# Design development record

23 September 2026

## Kresling

### 01 / Narrow seam

Seam interference. The full-height seam interfered with collapse, motivating a revised joint geometry.

### 02 / Relief cuts + small tabs

Fabrication constraint. Relief openings and short tabs complicated manual cutting and bonding. Subsequent designs used a continuous sheet.

### 03 / Two-piece full-facet overlap

Assembly revision. Full-triangle overlaps increased the bonding area. The 60 mm design required two sheets and two body joints; one piece is shown.

### 04 / One continuous body

Final physical prototype. A 43 mm rim edge and 30° print rotation placed the body on one Letter sheet. One full-triangle overlap closes the cylinder; caps are printed separately.

## Miura

### 01 / 8 × 8 facet sheet

Folding complexity. The sheet required extensive coordinated folding. A seeded defect persisted numerically, while direct loading and release returned to the initial state.

### 02 / 12-cell curved lens-box

Fabrication and response. Curved scoring and bonded connectors complicated fabrication. Full-panel loading did not produce switching; a smaller diagnostic patch did not complete the return transition.

### 03 / 6 × 6 straight facets

Refinement check. Straight scoring simplified fabrication, but a coarse-model second state disappeared on refinement. Other thin-sheet equilibria persisted; the finer switching calculation did not converge.

### 04 / One-vertex / 2 × 2 facets

Simplified trial. Four 45 mm rhombi reduced the folding effort. The initial model returned to A; revised stiffness assumptions produced a numerical cycle. The first physical trial was inconclusive.

### 05 / Diagonal panel hinges

Final physical prototype. Added diagonals through B and D divided two rhombi into four triangles. The 65 lb cardstock specimen exhibited A → B → A pop-through and retained both configurations after release.
